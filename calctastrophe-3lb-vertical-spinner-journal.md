Changed Wheel Diameter of front wheel to be 1.375" (reduced from 2") and 0.25" thicker (from 0.25" thick). This was a necessary change because I wanted more contact area on the front wheels and I did not torque bias at all, 1:1 ratio
I reworked the Wheel Guards to account for the wheels changing in diameter

- Thinned them out by creating an taper on the top, effectively reducing weight 

The Weapon System is the last step, but I have reworked it to use clamping collars instead of the simple set screw collars before.

- This change was made because I read online that the load applied to these set screw collars would shear the set screw and eventually lead to the clamping force not being significant.

# 11/17/2025 11 PM - Finished BOM and Finalized Shaft Design + Prep for Submit!  

_Time spent: 4.0h_  

Finalized the Weapon Shaft design to use spacers to prevent lateral movement and a shoulder bolt instead of a stainless steel shaft with two clamping shaft collars on it. 
- We previously had struggles with debating between just having a shaft press fit in between the two UHMW Uprights (decided not to because friction fit not a good choice when there is a high load part)
- We also decided to go away from the two clamping shaft collars because it was cheaper weight-wise to use a shoulder bolt

We also spent time researching load capacities and found that normal ball bearings would be far too weak for the load we would be applying on them from our weapon, so we decided to pivot to using needle roller bearings as they would be significantly stronger. 

BOM was also completed with the most accurate parts.
